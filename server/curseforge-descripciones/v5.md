Information:

Step into a world where creativity meets innovation. This project invites you to design, build, and automate with limitless potential. Featuring a foundation of advanced engineering tools, it empowers players to construct complex mechanisms powered by kinetic energy, creating a gameplay experience like no other.

Explore a vibrant, enhanced world filled with new biomes, structures, and discoveries that add depth and excitement to your adventures. With detailed visual improvements and thoughtful quality-of-life enhancements, this project ensures every moment is engaging and enjoyable.

Whether you’re a builder, inventor, or explorer, this project offers an ideal blend of aesthetics, functionality, and challenge to inspire your imagination.

Version 5.0.0!

Version 5.0.0 focuses on a renewed design and improved visual experience

This version is centered on delivering a more polished, clean, and visually consistent modpack, while maintaining good performance across a wide range of devices.

Among the main improvements:
Fully renewed visual and interface design
Improved aesthetic consistency between all mods
Cleaner menus, UI elements, and in-game visuals
Better color balance and overall atmosphere
Small visual refinements that improve immersion
Improved mod integration for a more unified experience
In addition to design improvements, we’ve also optimized:
Performance across all devices, including mid-range and low-end systems
Faster loading times compared to previous versions
Better compatibility between mods to reduce visual and functional conflicts
In addition to these improvements, we’ve also removed several things:
Removal of mods that caused instability or visual inconsistency
Fewer mods overall to improve performance and user experience
Adjustments to external feature integration to keep the modpack clean and stable
Improvements to troubleshooting:
Improved error detection features
Clearer information when requesting help
Better internal reporting to diagnose issues more easily
Known issues:
There may be performance drops during intensive moments depending on device
Lower-end systems may require reduced settings for stable gameplay
We will continue working to improve performance and optimization
Minimum Requirements
Technical Requirements:
Intel UHD Graphics or similar integrated graphics
Minimum 6GB of RAM allocated for gameplay (8GB installed on the PC recommended)
5GB to 10GB of free storage to install the modpack and create multiple worlds
Game Requirements (Recommended Settings):
Simulation and Rendering Distance: 4 – 8
Graphics: Fast
Particles: Minimum or Reduced
Dynamic Lighting: Disabled (or all options at minimum)
Minor but Important Changes
Cardinal System

The Cardinal System, our performance-enhancing and diagnostic system, has been improved.
You can find a “Cardinal System” button in the settings menu.

Don’t worry if you don’t understand the information shown there — we can help you interpret it when you request support from StormGamesStudios.
If you’re curious about how it works, feel free to ask us 😉
