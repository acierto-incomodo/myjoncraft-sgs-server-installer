
# MyJonCraft SGS

> **[Download on CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/modpacks/myjoncraft-sgs)**

Step into a world where creativity meets innovation. MyJonCraft SGS invites you to design, build, and automate with limitless potential. Featuring a powerful foundation of engineering tools centered around **Create** and its many addons, it empowers players to construct complex mechanisms, automate factories, and explore a world packed with content — delivering a gameplay experience like no other.

Explore a vibrant world filled with new biomes, dimensions, structures, mobs, and discoveries that add depth and excitement to your adventures. With detailed visual improvements, a rich selection of shaders, and thoughtful quality-of-life enhancements, every moment is engaging and enjoyable.

Whether you're a builder, inventor, or explorer, MyJonCraft SGS offers an ideal blend of aesthetics, functionality, and challenge to inspire your imagination.

All changes to the modpack are registered on the **MyJonCraft SGS Web** *(currently only in Spanish from Spain and may be discontinued due to minor problems)*.

## Minimum Requirements

| | Minimum | Recommended |
|---|---|---|
| **GPU** | Intel UHD Graphics or equivalent integrated GPU | Dedicated GPU (NVIDIA/AMD) |
| **RAM allocated** | 6 GB | 8–12 GB |
| **RAM installed** | 8 GB | 16+ GB |
| **Storage** | 2–5 GB free | 10+ GB free |

**Recommended in-game settings:**
- Simulation & Render Distance: 4–8
- Graphics: Fast
- Particles: Minimum or Reduced
- Dynamic Lighting: Disabled (or all options at minimum)

## Performance Tests

### Desktop Gaming PC
- **CPU:** AMD Ryzen 7 8700F 8-Core Processor | **GPU:** # ASUS DUAL GeForce RTX 5060 Ti OC Edition 16GB (16GB VRAM) | **RAM:** 32GB | **OS:** Windows 11 Pro
- Setup: Dedicated GPU, 32GB RAM allocated, Armoury Crate Turbo mode, Razer Cortex, Windows Game Mode
- **Result by Minecraft:** ~300+ FPS at minimum quality, render/simulation at 2 — GPU: 56–78% | RAM: 49–87% | CPU: 35–60%
- **Result by Razer Cortex:** AVG: ~164 FPS, MAX: 838 FPS, MIN: 0 FPS. All on max quality without shaders (Render distance, simulation distance and shadows distance at 32).

### Asus ROG Zephyrus (2018–2019 Laptop)
- **CPU:** AMD | **GPU:** NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660 Ti Max-Q (6GB VRAM) | **RAM:** 24GB | **OS:** Windows 11 / Windows 10 Enterprise
- Setup: Dedicated GPU, 16GB RAM allocated, Armoury Crate Turbo mode, Razer Cortex, Windows Game Mode
- **Result:** ~120 FPS at minimum quality, render/simulation at 8 — GPU: 4–30% | RAM: 40–100% | CPU: 80–95%*

> *The CPU on this unit came defective from the factory; results may differ on other units with the same specs.

### HP EliteBook 840 G7 (2019 Laptop)
- **CPU:** Intel Core i5-10310U | **GPU:** UHD Graphics 620 (128MB VRAM) | **RAM:** 16GB | **OS:** KDE neon User Edition v6.6.3
